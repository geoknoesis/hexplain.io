Explicit boundaries

Encoded repeat-until arrays remain rejected: no per-element framing is defined for that combination in this subset. Single encoded structs may contain supported plain bounded sequences. Unsupported codec options and encoded dispatch/layout combinations retain explicit rejections. This is not a general expression inversion solver, coverage-guided fuzz campaign, implementation of every GDAL driver or certification of every possible vocabulary combination.

Independent ontology review, human accessibility/print acceptance, remote CI and hosted OS containment remain external acceptance tasks. Live publication remains deferred and excluded from scoring at the user's request. No new production-readiness score or 10/10 claim is made.
