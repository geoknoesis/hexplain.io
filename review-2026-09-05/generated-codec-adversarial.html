Acceptance evidence

  • 180 combinations: six pipelines, ten lengths (0, 1, 2, 7, 31, 255, 256, 8191, 8192, 8193), and three patterns (zero-filled, periodic and seeded pseudorandom). Seed: 20260910.
  • Independent JDK compression constructs expected bytes. Compiled generated writers and the interpreter writer match them exactly, including inferred lengths. Both readers recover the expected payload; generated readers preserve the following trailer.
  • For a four-byte payload in each pipeline, every strict compressed prefix rejects. Tests rewrite the enclosing length so rejection exercises codec framing rather than an outer short read. Appended compressed-region junk also rejects in both implementations.
  • A repeated two-stage codec succeeds exactly at the sum of its intermediate and final decoded lengths and rejects a budget one byte smaller.

The codegen-verify check passes: 50 tests, zero failures, errors or skips. The check includes compilation against the standalone runtime; unchanged generated sources reused their prior successful compilation outputs.

Remaining scope

This is a bounded deterministic adversarial corpus, not coverage-guided fuzzing or complete malformed-format coverage. It does not add codec capabilities, support encoded nested structs or repeated encoded fields, establish independent ontology acceptance, or certify release readiness. Larger campaigns and additional format families remain separate work.
