Hexplain Aspect — Time (hx-time)

Orthogonal aspect for the temporal extent and structure of data (duration, and — as the aspect grows — timebase, frame count, timecode). Imported by any domain whose data has a timeline.

Datatype property

atime:duration — duration

IRI: https://hexplain.io/ns/aspect/time#duration

Label
duration
Definition
The elapsed playing time of content expressed in seconds. It is a temporal extent, distinct from a timestamp or a count of frames or samples.
Usage scope
Use for temporal metadata of content. Numeric durations and frame/sample counts are not interchangeable without a timing model. Use it as a predicate. Applicable SHACL paths and their focus-node scopes are listed below; no additional global domain is inferred from that usage.
