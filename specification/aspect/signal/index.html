asig:SampleRateShape — Positive sample rate

IRI: https://hexplain.io/ns/aspect/signal#SampleRateShape

Label
Positive sample rate
Definition
A stated sample rate is a single positive decimal or integer-family value in hertz. Decimal rates admit exact finite fractional rates such as 0.5 Hz; binary floating-point literals and rounded rational approximations must be converted explicitly by the profile.

Datatype property

asig:sampleRate — sample rate

IRI: https://hexplain.io/ns/aspect/signal#sampleRate

Label
sample rate
Definition
The number of sample instants per second for a sampled signal, expressed in hertz. Channel count is separate; a multi-channel sample instant is not multiplied by its number of channels.
Usage scope
Use for sampled signals; state channel organization and units separately from sample rate. Use it as a predicate. Applicable SHACL paths and their focus-node scopes are listed below; no additional global domain is inferred from that usage.
