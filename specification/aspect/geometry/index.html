Hexplain specification

Geometry aspect 1.4

Working draft · modified 2026-10-14

Canonical TurtleGDAL coverageModeling policy

Geometry properties are independent of container layout and CRS. Profiles bind geometry-type concepts, allowing curves, surfaces, collections and format extensions without adding format-specific terms to this aspect.

Ordinate semantics

The independent hasZ and hasM flags distinguish XY, XYZ, XYM and XYZM. Missing flags mean unknown, not false. A measure ordinate MUST NOT be treated as elevation or time without an explicit semantic mapping. Legacy dimensionality values alone cannot distinguish XYZ from XYM; importers SHOULD emit both flags when the source carries them.

Reference: GDAL measured geometry model. No geometry parser or topology engine is introduced by these properties.

Term reference

Geometry
One encoded geometric object: a single geometry of one geometry type (ageom:geometryType) and one ordinate set (ageom:dimensionality, ageom:hasZ, ageom:hasM). A multi-geometry or a geometry collection is one geometry whose parts are its members (ageom:hasMember).
GeometryRecord
A stored geometry value with the framing its container adds: a header that may state a spatial reference, an envelope or an emptiness flag, around exactly one encoded geometry (ageom:hasGeometry).
coordinatePrecision
Number of decimal places retained per coordinate.
dimensionality
Coordinate dimensionality (2 for XY, 3 for XYZ).
geometryType
Kind of geometry, from a geometry-type register bound by the profile.
hasGeometry
Links a geometry record to the one geometry it encodes.
hasM
Whether a geometry contains an M measure ordinate. A measure is not implicitly elevation or time. XYM and XYZ are distinct despite having three ordinates.
hasMember
Links a multi-geometry or geometry collection to one of its member geometries, a part that belongs to that collection alone. Member order is not conveyed by this property.
hasZ
Whether a geometry contains a Z ordinate. Independent of a measure ordinate; missing means unknown, not false.
