Specification family

Hexplain Requirements Vocabulary

Identifies requirements stated by an external standards document, so that executable conformance constraints can cite them. Format-agnostic: it describes what documents demand, independent of any binary format description.

Unofficial working specification. The namespace and version metadata below identify the vocabulary; documentation does not certify an implementation.

Complete term and shape reference

12 named resources are documented below. Definitions and usage notes come from the canonical RDF; range and validation facts are rendered from its axioms and shapes. RDFS/OWL inference, SHACL validation, and editorial usage guidance are shown separately. Blank-node property shapes and logical alternatives are expanded under their owning named shapes.

Constraint language follows W3C SHACL; publication and persistent term identifiers follow the W3C vocabulary publication guidance. Concept definitions and scope notes use SKOS documentation properties.

Definition
