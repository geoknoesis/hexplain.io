Hexplain Conformance Vocabulary

Binds executable HEL assertions to the standards requirements they check. Format-agnostic. Severity is deliberately absent: it derives from the cited requirement's req:discrepancyType, so a rule reused across profiles is classified consistently.

Unofficial working specification. The namespace and version metadata below identify the vocabulary; documentation does not certify an implementation.

<https://hexplain.io/ns/conf> — Hexplain Conformance Vocabulary

IRI: https://hexplain.io/ns/conf

Label
Hexplain Conformance Vocabulary
Definition
Binds executable HEL assertions to the standards requirements they check, and describes the findings and coverage a conformance run reports. Format-agnostic. Severity: a constraint reports at its conf:severity (sh:Violation, sh:Warning or sh:Info), sh:Violation when absent. req:discrepancyType classifies the defect (syntactic, semantic, functional); it never sets or modifies severity. A constraint citing several requirements reports ONE finding per failed evaluation, citing all of them, at the constraint's severity. Declared coverage of a profile: the set of requirements cited (conf:satisfies) by at least one conf:Constraint in the profile. It is a property of the profile and needs no input. Run coverage: for one run over one input, each requirement receives exactly one conf:EvaluationOutcome, computed from the evaluations of the constraints that cite it -- conf:NotApplicable, conf:Errored, conf:Evaluated or conf:NotExercised, with that precedence (see conf:EvaluationOutcome).
Usage scope
Use for constraints evaluated against a declared structural scope and attributed to requirements. A requirement is not an executable assertion by itself.

conf:EvaluationOutcome — Evaluation Outcome

IRI: https://hexplain.io/ns/conf#EvaluationOutcome

Label
Evaluation Outcome
Definition
The run-coverage outcome of one requirement in one conformance run. Closed set of four. Given the constraints citing the requirement, the outcome is the FIRST of these that holds: (1) conf:NotApplicable -- the requirement's req:appliesToVersion values are present and none matches the input's format version (an input whose version cannot be determined matches every version; constraint evaluations are then not considered); (2) conf:Errored -- evaluating at least one citing constraint raised a rule error; (3) conf:Evaluated -- at least one citing constraint was evaluated, without error, on at least one scoped instance; (4) conf:NotExercised -- no scoped instance of any citing constraint occurred in the input. A requirement cited by no constraint is outside declared coverage and receives no outcome.

conf:Finding — Finding

IRI: https://hexplain.io/ns/conf#Finding

Label
Finding
Definition
One reported discrepancy of a conformance run: a failed evaluation of a constraint (conf:Violation) or a constraint whose evaluation could not complete (conf:RuleError), attributed to every requirement the constraint cites and located in the input. A failed evaluation yields exactly one finding however many requirements the constraint cites. A finding raised by parse recovery has no constraint and reports at sh:Violation; otherwise its severity is its constraint's conf:severity.
