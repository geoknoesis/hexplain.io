Evidence held privately

The rest of the review's evidence concerns the proprietary reference engine and the hosted service rather than the specification: engine test runs (including the full core and adapter verification of the vector-geometry work and the Jena security-mutation test run), engine source excerpts and probes, the hosted service's backend, tenancy, worker and CI logs, and the review report that discussed them. It is held privately with the engine and is not published. The specification's own claims do not depend on it: each is backed by a public gate or by the files above.
