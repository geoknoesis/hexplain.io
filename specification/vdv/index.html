The Hexplain Video Vocabulary (VDV) provides standard terms for the semantic properties of video data. Used with BDDO, DLV, and Hexplain Core, it maps low-level fields from container formats (MXF, MOV, MP4, AVI) to high-level concepts such as frame rate, codec, resolution, and duration, enabling interoperability and semantic querying across video formats regardless of container. It owl:imports the aspect layer and reuses its orthogonal terms — aenc:codec/aenc:bitrate (encoding), acolor:colorSpace (colour), atime:duration (time) — rather than redefining them. Prefix: vdv; namespace: https://hexplain.io/ns/video#.

This document is an unofficial draft produced for the purpose of defining the Hexplain standard. It has no official standing and is not endorsed by any standards body. This is the first version.

Introduction

Video files combine encoded picture and sound streams with technical metadata inside a container. BDDO describes the container's physical structure and DLV the layout of frame data; VDV adds the meaning of fields like "frame rate" or "codec," enabling quality-control queries such as "flag all files whose vdv:frameRate is not 29.97 fps or whose audio channel count is not 2."

Conformance

The key words "MUST", "SHOULD", "MAY", etc. are to be interpreted as in [[RFC2119]].

Core Concepts (Classes)

vdv:VideoContainer
The video file/container as a whole (MP4, MOV…).
vdv:VideoStream
The encoded picture stream.
vdv:VideoTrack
A track within the container.

Semantic Properties

Controlled Vocabularies

Codec and colour space are object properties whose values are skos:Concept members of open registers, rather than free-text strings. These registers are standalone documents bound by the profile via hexplain:usesRegister — menc:CodecScheme and rcol:ColorSpaceScheme — and are shared with the audio and image vocabularies, so a codec carries the same IRI (e.g. menc:H264) regardless of media type. By contrast, vdv:aspectRatio remains a literal because it is a computable value (a W:H ratio), not a term; its form is constrained by SHACL.

Example: _:stream aenc:codec menc:H264 ; acolor:colorSpace rcol:BT709 ; vdv:aspectRatio "16:9" .

Layout Axes

VDV adds the temporal axis vdv:axisFrame for a sequence of frames (see dlv:axisTime); spatial axes reuse dlv:axisX/dlv:axisY and the channel axis reuses dlv:axisBand.

Example of Usage

@prefix bddo:     <https://hexplain.io/ns/bddo#> .
@prefix hexplain: <https://hexplain.io/ns/core#> .
@prefix vdv:      <https://hexplain.io/ns/video#> .
@prefix araster:  <https://hexplain.io/ns/aspect/raster#> .
@prefix mp4:      <https://hexplain.io/formats/mp4#> .

# The MP4 'tkhd' (track header) mapped to a VideoStream.
mp4:TrackHeader a bddo:Struct ;
    hexplain:mapsToClass vdv:VideoStream ;
    bddo:endianness bddo:BigEndian ;
    bddo:hasField ( mp4:Width mp4:Height ) .

mp4:Width  a bddo:Field ; bddo:dataType bddo:uint32 ; hexplain:mapsToProperty araster:width .
mp4:Height a bddo:Field ; bddo:dataType bddo:uint32 ; hexplain:mapsToProperty araster:height .
