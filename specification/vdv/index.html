The Hexplain Video Vocabulary (VDV) provides standard terms for the semantic properties of video data. Used with BDDO, DLV, and Hexplain Core, it maps low-level fields from container formats (MXF, MOV, MP4, AVI) to high-level concepts such as frame rate, codec, resolution, and duration, enabling interoperability and semantic querying across video formats regardless of container. Prefix: vdv; namespace: https://hexplain.io/ns/video#.

This document is an unofficial draft produced for the purpose of defining the Hexplain standard. It has no official standing and is not endorsed by any standards body. This is the first version. (Earlier drafts of the family listed a "VDV" module whose vdv/ path erroneously contained the Document & Font vocabulary; that content now lives at dfv/, and this document is the real Video Vocabulary.)

Introduction

Video files combine encoded picture and sound streams with technical metadata inside a container. BDDO describes the container's physical structure and DLV the layout of frame data; VDV adds the meaning of fields like "frame rate" or "codec," enabling quality-control queries such as "flag all files whose vdv:frameRate is not 29.97 fps or whose audio channel count is not 2."

Conformance

The key words "MUST", "SHOULD", "MAY", etc. are to be interpreted as in [[RFC2119]].

Core Concepts (Classes)

vdv:VideoContainer
The video file/container as a whole (MP4, MOV…).
vdv:VideoStream
The encoded picture stream.
vdv:VideoTrack
A track within the container.

Semantic Properties

PropertyDescriptionRange
vdv:frameWidth, vdv:frameHeightPicture dimensions in pixels.xsd:positiveInteger
vdv:frameRateFrames per second.xsd:decimal
vdv:durationDuration in seconds.xsd:decimal
vdv:bitrateAverage bitrate (bits/s).xsd:positiveInteger
vdv:codecIdentifierCodec name ("H.264", "ProRes", "VP9").xsd:string
vdv:aspectRatioDisplay aspect ratio ("16:9").xsd:string
vdv:colorSpaceColour space ("BT.709", "BT.2020").xsd:string
vdv:scanTypeProgressive or interlaced.vdv:Progressive/vdv:Interlaced
vdv:frameCountTotal number of frames.xsd:unsignedLong
vdv:audioChannelsAudio channel count of the muxed audio.xsd:positiveInteger

Layout Axes

VDV adds the temporal axis vdv:axisFrame for a sequence of frames (see dlv:axisTime); spatial axes reuse dlv:axisX/dlv:axisY and the channel axis reuses dlv:axisBand.

Example of Usage

@prefix bddo:     <https://hexplain.io/ns/bddo#> .
@prefix hexplain: <https://hexplain.io/ns/core#> .
@prefix vdv:      <https://hexplain.io/ns/video#> .
@prefix mp4:      <https://hexplain.io/formats/mp4#> .

# The MP4 'tkhd' (track header) mapped to a VideoStream.
mp4:TrackHeader a bddo:Struct ;
    hexplain:mapsToClass vdv:VideoStream ;
    bddo:endianness bddo:BigEndian ;
    bddo:hasField ( mp4:Width mp4:Height ) .

mp4:Width  a bddo:Field ; bddo:dataType bddo:uint32 ; hexplain:mapsToProperty vdv:frameWidth .
mp4:Height a bddo:Field ; bddo:dataType bddo:uint32 ; hexplain:mapsToProperty vdv:frameHeight .
