Complete reference: 13 terms and shapes · All vocabularies and reading guide

The Hexplain Audio Vocabulary (ADV) provides standard terms for the semantic properties of audio data. Used with BDDO and Hexplain Core, it maps fields from formats like WAV or MP3 (ID3) to concepts such as sample rate, artist, and album. It owl:imports the aspect layer and reuses its orthogonal terms — aenc:codec/aenc:bitrate (encoding) and atime:duration (time) — rather than redefining them. Prefix: adv; namespace: https://hexplain.io/ns/audio#.

This document is an unofficial draft produced for the purpose of defining the Hexplain standard. It has no official standing and is not endorsed by any standards body.

Introduction

Audio files carry both technical data (the encoded stream) and descriptive metadata (tags). BDDO describes the physical structure; ADV adds the meaning of fields like "sample rate" or "artist," enabling queries such as "find all audio by 'The Beatles' at 44100 Hz."

Conformance

The key words "MUST", "SHOULD", "MAY", etc. are to be interpreted as in [[RFC2119]].

Core Concepts (Classes)

adv:AudioContainer
The audio file as a whole (WAV, MP3…).
adv:AudioStream
The encoded audio sample data.
adv:MetadataTag
A descriptive tag block (ID3, Vorbis comment).

Layout Axes

ADV adds audio-specific axes to DLV: adv:axisSample (often corresponds to time; see dlv:axisTime) and adv:axisChannel (for interleaved channels; see dlv:axisBand).

Example of Usage

@prefix bddo:     <https://hexplain.io/ns/bddo#> .
@prefix hexplain: <https://hexplain.io/ns/core#> .
@prefix adv:      <https://hexplain.io/ns/audio#> .
@prefix asamp:    <https://hexplain.io/ns/aspect/sampling#> .
@prefix asig:     <https://hexplain.io/ns/aspect/signal#> .
@prefix wav:      <https://hexplain.io/formats/wav#> .

wav:FormatChunk a bddo:Struct ;
    hexplain:mapsToClass adv:AudioStream ;
    bddo:endianness bddo:LittleEndian ;
    bddo:hasField ( wav:SampleRate wav:Channels wav:BitsPerSample ) .

wav:SampleRate a bddo:Field ; bddo:dataType bddo:uint32 ; hexplain:mapsToProperty asig:sampleRate .
wav:Channels a bddo:Field ; bddo:dataType bddo:uint16 ; hexplain:mapsToProperty asamp:componentCount .
wav:BitsPerSample a bddo:Field ; bddo:dataType bddo:uint16 ; hexplain:mapsToProperty asamp:bitDepth .
