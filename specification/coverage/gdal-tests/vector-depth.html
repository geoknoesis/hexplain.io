Defect found and corrected

GDAL's XYZM GeoPackage data used an XYZ envelope. Hexplain incorrectly required identical envelope and geometry dimensions. It now validates the indicated subset; XYM envelopes index the M ordinate rather than Z. Independent boundary tests retain rejection when M lies outside its envelope.

Ontology mapping

The geometry-type register now includes GeometryCollection, completing the seven measured simple-feature concepts. GeometryType identifies the kind; hasZ and hasM preserve ordinate meaning. An M value does not implicitly mean time, and a CRS identifier is not proof that a coordinate transformation occurred.

GeoPackage stores coordinates in x,y,z,m order independently of the CRS authority's axis order. Envelope indicators describe XY, XYZ, XYM or XYZM bounds, per the OGC GeoPackage geometry encoding contract. Oracle generation uses the GDAL vector API.
